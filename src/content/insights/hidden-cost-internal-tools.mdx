Internal tools are often treated as back-office afterthoughts. That choice has a price.

## Death by a thousand clicks

When operators re-key data, work around broken flows, or rely on spreadsheets, you pay in:

- **Time** — slower throughput per employee
- **Errors** — costly corrections downstream
- **Morale** — attrition among your best operators

## The ROI of operator experience

Investing in internal product design isn't charity—it's leverage. A 20% efficiency gain across a 50-person ops team compounds fast.

## Takeaway

Your customer experience is only as good as the systems your team uses to deliver it. Design internal tools with the same bar as external ones.
