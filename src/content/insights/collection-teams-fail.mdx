Most collection teams don't fail because they lack software. They fail because their **processes were never designed**—only digitized.

## The process-first lens

Before recommending a new dashboard or CRM, I map:

- How accounts enter the queue
- Who owns each transition
- Where data gets re-keyed
- What leadership actually needs to decide

## What changes when you design the system

When workflow clarity comes first, tools become accelerators—not band-aids. Collection OS emerged from this exact pattern: visibility for leadership, speed for agents, and fewer handoff errors in between.

## Takeaway

If your collection metrics aren't moving, audit the process before you audit the UI. The best interface in the world can't fix a broken operational model.
