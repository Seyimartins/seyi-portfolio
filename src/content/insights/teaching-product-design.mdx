Teaching product design to 60+ students forced me to explain **why** we design—not just how.

## Clarity beats cleverness

Students who succeeded weren't the most visual—they were the most clear. They could articulate the problem, the user, and the success metric before opening Figma.

## Constraints accelerate learning

Real briefs with real constraints (time, data, stakeholders) produced better portfolios than open-ended "redesign Netflix" exercises.

## Feedback is a product skill

Critique isn't personal—it's iterative. The best designers treat feedback like user research: listen, synthesize, decide.

## Takeaway

Mentoring sharpened my own practice. If you can't teach it simply, you don't understand it deeply enough yet.
